# V0.3.1 Post-Experiment Semantic Evaluator Audit

The frozen V0.3 automated release decision remains **FAIL**. This audit does not replace it.

All 60 clean generated replies were independently reviewed for unsupported material factual claims. Human-confirmed support was 29/30 (96.7%) for baseline and 21/30 (70.0%) for candidate. The local judge agreed with the human audit on 16/30 baseline replies (53.3%) and 13/30 candidate replies (43.3%). It produced 24 false positives, 6 false negatives, and one unavailable candidate judgment.

## Interpretation

The semantic judge is **unreliable for release authority in this bounded experiment**. It often treated restated customer-provided facts as unsupported, while missing several unsupported operational commitments or claims. The historical V0.3 automated FAIL remains an accurate record of what the frozen system decided, but its public interpretation is materially limited by evaluator reliability.
